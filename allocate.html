Why this order

What the solver is comparing

Each dollar is scored against what it earns you this year, then handed to whichever account scores highest, and the scoring is redone as your income falls:

  • Employer match — the match rate itself, on top of the deduction. Nothing competes with an instant 50%.
  • FHSA — your full effective marginal rate, because it is deductible going in and tax-free coming out for a first home.
  • RRSP — your effective rate today minus the rate you expect to withdraw at. That difference is the whole bet, and it goes negative when you are contributing at a lower rate than you will withdraw at.
  • TFSA — no deduction, so it scores zero, but it is never negative. It is the baseline every other account has to beat.
  • Regular account — only once every registered dollar of room is used.
